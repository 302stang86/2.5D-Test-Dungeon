2.5D Test Dungeon

How to Play

Recover the main supply crate and return to the entrance.

Use WASD to move, drag to aim, F to interact, Space or click to attack, G to equip or stow, R to reload, and H to use a bandage. Press Esc to return to the menu.

Play in landscape. Move with the joystick and drag the view to aim. Hold Crouch to stay low and stationary while you use the action buttons.
